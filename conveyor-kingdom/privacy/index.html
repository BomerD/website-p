Privacy policy

Conveyor Kingdom: Color Loop · Last updated 10 October 2026

Conveyor Kingdom: Color Loop does not collect your personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no ads. Your progress is stored on your device and never sent to us.

Who we are

Conveyor Kingdom: Color Loop (Android package com.bomerd.conveyorkingdom) is made by Bomerd. You can reach us at bomerrd@gmail.com.

What the game stores on your device

The game saves the following locally, in its private app storage on your device:

  • Which levels you have cleared.
  • Your coin balance and the levels of your upgrades.
  • Your chosen shooter and the Keeper's outfit.
  • Whether you have opened the Upgrades screen since new upgrades became available.

This never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not included in device backups. Uninstalling the game deletes all of it.

What we do not do

  • No internet connection. The game does not request internet access from Android.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email collection.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising services.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

The only Android permission the game uses is vibration, for short feedback buzzes during play.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data. Because the game holds nothing about you on our side, there is nothing for us to hand over or erase: your game data is on your device, and uninstalling the game deletes it. If you have a question, write to bomerrd@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Conveyor Kingdom: Color Loop is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children, and it does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date at the top. If a future version of the game adds anything that involves personal data, such as optional ads, this policy will be updated before that version reaches you.

Contact

Questions, requests or complaints about this policy: bomerrd@gmail.com
