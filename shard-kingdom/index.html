Shard Kingdom: Color Defense

Try it: tap the color of the closest crystal.

Glass crystals march down the road toward your tower, each a stack of gems of one color. Tap a color and your gun fires it at the closest crystal of that color. Fire the right ones, shatter the glass, hold the wall.

How it plays

One tap, one shot. Every shot counts.

Tap a color

Your gun fires it at once at the closest crystal of that color. The wrong color smashes on the road, and three misses at once jam the gun.

The Keeper refills

Every color has its own stock. When one runs dry, the Keeper runs to his vials for more while your other colors keep firing. Tap him to send him for all of them.

Load the special

Twelve hits in a row load your gun's special. Fire it at the color you choose. A miss empties the bar.

Hold the wall

Crystals that reach their line throw shards at your wall. Break them first, and the wall mends a little after every phase.

Every gem has a color and a shape

So colors are easy to tell apart, even for players who see them differently. A crystal is a stack of one gem: every gem of its color you fire knocks one off.

Pick your gun

Three guns, three ways to play. Pick the one that suits how you tap, and switch any time.

Cannon

Steady

The most forgiving, for players who miss now and then.

Knockback
Every hit shoves the crystal back down the road.
Big Shot
A cannonball breaks a crystal of your color and blasts every crystal in range back.

Beacon

Precise

Reaches farther, and its beams land at once. For players who rarely miss.

Focus
Every fifth hit in a row is a focused beam that knocks off two gems.
Flash
Breaks a crystal and freezes every crystal of its color.

Organ

Fast

The quickest notes, for fast fingers.

Chords
Every third hit of a quick run also strikes the next crystal of its color.
Fanfare
A run of notes, one after another, at the color you choose.

Six abilities

Take two into every level, for when the Storm gets too close. Each has five levels to buy, and each gun has one more of its own.

Freeze

Every crystal stands still in a block of ice, and none come for a few seconds.

Shield

Light runs along the wall and gives it HP on top of its full strength.

Spark

Bolts from the gun break the crystals closest to the wall at once.

Earthquake

The ground shakes and every crystal in range loses gems.

Repair

The wall's cracks mend, up to most of its strength.

Gust

A wave of wind runs up the road and blows the crystals back.

Questions

About Shard Kingdom: Color Defense.

Is Shard Kingdom free?

Yes. It's free on Google Play. Its ads are few: a short one as each new world opens, and ones you choose to watch for a reward, such as a Revive or double coins. The Shop sells packs of crowns and coins and a few unique outfits for the Keeper; everything else is earned by playing.

Is it on iPhone?

Not yet. Shard Kingdom: Color Defense is on Android, through Google Play.

Does it work offline?

Yes. There's no account and no sign-in, and every level and mode plays without a connection. Ads and purchases need one.

How many levels are there?

60, ten in each of the six worlds, every one played in phases. Then Infinite and Rush go on for as long as you can hold the wall.

How do I unlock Infinite and Rush?

Clear Dawn, the first world (levels 1 to 10), and Infinite opens. Clear Mist, the second (levels 11 to 20), and Rush opens. Both are on the main menu, a swipe to either side of the levels.

Which gun should I pick?

The Cannon if you miss now and then, the Beacon if you rarely miss, the Organ if you tap fast. You can switch any time on the Upgrades screen.

Will I lose my progress if I reinstall?

Your progress is saved on your phone, so uninstalling the game deletes it. Unique outfits you bought come back with Restore purchases in the game's Settings.
